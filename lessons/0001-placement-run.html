How the game works

The map has one region per chapter of Designing Data-Intensive Applications, 2nd edition. Each region has short levels (10–15 minutes each), a side quest you do at work, and a boss: an interview-style fight.

Every level starts with a Recall Run of cards from earlier levels. Those cards come back on a schedule in the Daily Review. Pulling an idea from memory a few days later builds lasting memory. Rereading only makes it feel familiar.[1]

Today's job is easier. Answer what you know, and press Not sure yet for anything else. A guess hides what you don't know, and then the lessons start at the wrong level.
