Spaced repetition

🃏 Daily Review

Cards from every level you've cleared, mixed across chapters. A card you get right waits longer before it comes back: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then 32 days. A card you miss starts again at one day.

Why this works: pulling an idea from memory, spaced over days and mixed with other topics, builds memory that lasts much longer than rereading.[1][2] It feels harder, and that difficulty is what does the work.

💬
Keep missing the same card? Tell Claude which one. A card that keeps failing usually means the lesson needs another angle, not more repetition.
